Brixx Ads Manager

An internal campaign-management tool from Brixx Media LLC for creating, updating and reviewing Google Ads campaigns in accounts we are authorized to manage.

For authorized Brixx Media operators

What the app does

01 / CAMPAIGNS

Build and manage campaigns

Prepare campaigns, ad groups, keywords, ads and assets. Apply approved budgets, targeting and status changes through the Google Ads API.

02 / REPORTING

Review performance

Read campaign settings and reporting metrics, including impressions, clicks, cost and conversions, to support advertising decisions.

03 / WORKFLOW

Support daily operations

Use local scripts and AI-assisted workflows, including Codex, to prepare changes, inspect results and maintain campaign records.

How access works

Operators authorize the application through Google's OAuth consent flow. The app uses the Google Ads permission to read and manage advertising accounts available to the authorizing Google account. It does not ask for your Google password.

This is an internal operational integration, not a public self-service sign-up website. Account connections and campaign actions are handled by authorized operators. The public pages explain the application and its data practices.

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account connections. Removing access does not automatically pause existing campaigns or delete records already stored; contact us for assistance.

Read our app privacy policy for the data accessed, service-provider processing, retention and deletion instructions.
