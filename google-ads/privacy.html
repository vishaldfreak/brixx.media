Brixx Ads Manager

Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy describes how Brixx Media LLC (“Brixx Media”, “we” or “us”) handles information through Brixx Ads Manager, our internal Google Ads campaign-management and reporting integration. It also covers the public application-information pages under www.brixx.media/google-ads/.

1. Information the app accesses

When an authorized operator connects a Google account, the app can access the Google Ads accounts that account is permitted to manage. Depending on the requested workflow, information includes:

  • Advertising account identifiers, names, currency, time zone and account configuration.
  • Campaigns, budgets, bidding settings, ad groups, ads, keywords, targeting and advertising assets.
  • Reporting data such as impressions, clicks, advertising cost, conversion counts and conversion value.
  • Conversion-action configuration and, where a conversion workflow is enabled, associated click or transaction identifiers, timestamps and conversion values.
  • OAuth authorization credentials, request results, error messages, change receipts and operational logs needed to run and troubleshoot the integration.

The app does not receive your Google password. Google Ads authorization does not grant this app access to your Gmail messages, Drive files or Contacts.

2. How information is used

We use this information to carry out authorized campaign-management actions, prepare and review advertising changes, produce performance reports, maintain conversion configuration, troubleshoot failures and preserve records of changes. We do not sell Google user data or use it to build unrelated user profiles.

Brixx Ads Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Processing and sharing

Information is accessible to authorized Brixx Media operators and to service providers used to perform the requested workflow. Google processes API requests. When operators use AI-assisted tools such as OpenAI's Codex, relevant campaign information, instructions and API results may be processed by that provider to assist with the requested task. Hosting and automation providers may process information necessary to operate connected workflows.

Transfers must be limited to what is necessary for the application's campaign-management and reporting features and consistent with applicable Google data-use requirements. Brixx Media does not use Google user data to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to address fraud, abuse or security issues.

4. Storage and protection

Working files, reports, configuration and change receipts may be stored on Brixx Media-controlled workstations and within the configured service-provider systems used by operators. Credentials are used for authorized API access and are not part of this public website. Access is limited to authorized operators and systems. Connections to Google's API use HTTPS.

These public information pages do not contain advertising-account data or OAuth credentials. The pages contain no advertising pixels or analytics scripts. The website host may process standard web request information, such as IP address and browser details, to serve and protect the site.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed for the authorized advertising workflow, operational records, troubleshooting and applicable legal obligations. Removing Google authorization prevents future authorized API access but does not by itself delete existing reports, logs or campaign records.

To request access, correction or deletion of data held by Brixx Media, email vish@brixx.media and identify the connected account or advertising account. Do not send passwords, access tokens or client secrets. We may verify your authority over the account before acting. We will explain any information we must retain for legal or security reasons. Data within Google's services remains subject to Google's controls and policies.

6. Your choices

You can revoke the application's access in your Google Account's third-party connections. You can also change Google Ads account permissions or ask Brixx Media to disconnect the integration. Revocation does not stop campaigns that are already running; campaign status must be changed separately in Google Ads or by an authorized operator.

7. Changes and contact

We will publish changes to this policy here and update the effective date. Material changes to Google data use may require a new notice or authorization before they take effect.
